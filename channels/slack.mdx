title: Slack

ClawHQ integrates with Slack in two directions:

- **Outbound** — budget alerts and agent health notifications sent to a webhook URL (Settings → Notifications)
- **Inbound** — @mention your ClawHQ bot in any channel to dispatch a task to an agent and receive the reply in-thread

## Create a Slack app

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    Go to [api.slack.com/apps](https://api.slack.com/apps) and click **Create New App → From scratch**.

    Name it (e.g. "ClawHQ") and select your workspace.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set OAuth scopes">
    In the sidebar, click **OAuth & Permissions → Scopes → Bot Token Scopes**.

    Add these scopes:
    - `app_mentions:read`
    - `channels:history`
    - `channels:read`
    - `chat:write`
    - `im:history`
    - `im:read`
    - `im:write`
    - `users:read`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Event Subscriptions">
    Go to **Event Subscriptions** and toggle it on.

    Set the Request URL to:

    ```
    https://your-clawhq-domain.com/api/slack/events
    ```

    Slack will send a `url_verification` challenge — ClawHQ handles it automatically once your signing secret is configured (next step).

    Subscribe to these bot events:
    - `app_mention`
    - `message.im`
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy your credentials">
    **Bot Token** — go to **OAuth & Permissions** and click **Install to Workspace**, then copy the **Bot User OAuth Token** (`xoxb-...`).

    **Signing Secret** — go to **Basic Information → App Credentials** and copy the **Signing Secret**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add credentials to ClawHQ">
    In the ClawHQ dashboard:

    1. Go to **Settings → Integrations → Slack** and paste your **Bot Token**, then save.
    2. Go to **Settings → Notifications** and paste your **Signing Secret** into the **Slack Signing Secret** field.
    3. Toggle **Enable inbound @mentions** on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Trigger agents with @mentions

Mention your bot in any channel to dispatch a task:

```
@ClawHQ summarize the last 20 messages in this thread
@ClawHQ [Scout] research our top competitor's pricing page
@ClawHQ [Hermes] draft a reply to this customer complaint
```

The `[AgentName]` prefix is optional. If omitted, the task goes to the first running agent. The reply posts in the same thread within seconds.

## Outbound notifications

To receive budget alerts and health notifications in Slack without setting up a full app, you only need a webhook URL:

1. Create an [Incoming Webhook](https://api.slack.com/messaging/webhooks) for your workspace.
2. Paste the URL into **Settings → Notifications → Slack Webhook URL**.

The webhook is separate from the bot token — you can use either or both.

## Troubleshooting

**Events not arriving**
- Verify your ClawHQ URL is publicly accessible (not `localhost`)
- Check the Event Subscriptions page for delivery errors
- Ensure all required scopes are added and the app is reinstalled after adding scopes

**Requests rejected with 403**
- Confirm the Signing Secret in Settings matches the one in **Basic Information → App Credentials**
- Check that your server clock is accurate — requests older than 5 minutes are rejected to prevent replay attacks

**"not_authed" errors in replies**
- Your Bot Token may have expired — reinstall the app to get a fresh token
